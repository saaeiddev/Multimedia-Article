Multimedia as a digital ecosystem

Multimedia is the purposeful integration of multiple forms of media within a unified experience. Its contemporary scope reaches far beyond traditional content production, connecting design, software, interaction and emerging technologies.

The digital experience is increasingly intelligent, interactive, personalized and spatial.

Where multimedia is applied

Education, healthcare, engineering, simulation, entertainment and scientific visualization all use multimedia systems to communicate information and create richer interactions.

Looking forward

AI-driven creation, immersive interfaces, digital twins and spatial computing are reshaping how people create, learn, work and experience digital content.
